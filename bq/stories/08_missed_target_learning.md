# Resilience / Commercial: Missed an AUM target, diagnosed, exceeded next year

## Situation

Year-end `[20XX]`, I missed my NNM (net new money) target of `[US$50M]` by a material margin — ended at `[US$32M]`, around 64% of target. My variable compensation was impacted, and more painfully, I had to explain the miss in the annual review. No single catastrophic event caused it; it was a slow-motion shortfall over 12 months that I hadn't diagnosed in time.

## Task

Own the miss publicly with my team, do an honest diagnosis of the *cause* (not just the symptom of low AUM inflow), and restructure the next year's approach to actually deliver. Avoid either the "bad luck" narrative or the "just work harder" cop-out — both feel good but neither changes outcomes.

## Action

1. **Structured honest diagnosis, not post-hoc rationalisation.** I spent a December weekend analysing every prospect I'd worked in the year: 47 prospects, 12 converted, 35 didn't. For each non-conversion, categorised: (a) never had a real chance; (b) lost to a competitor — why; (c) decision delayed — why; (d) my process failures.
2. **Found the pattern.** 20 of 35 non-conversions clustered in a specific segment — mid-HNW (US$3-8M) professionals considering their first PB. My conversion rate in that segment was `[10%]` vs firm average `[22%]`. I was under-resourcing those prospects because the ticket size seemed less attractive than UHNW prospects, but my effort-to-close in UHNW was so high that the UHNW conversions also suffered.
3. **Changed the pipeline model.** Year `[N+1]` plan: (a) build a structured first-meeting process for mid-HNW prospects that respects their time but demonstrates depth; (b) re-weight time allocation — 60% UHNW prospecting (high-value), 40% mid-HNW (volume-critical) — rather than the 80/20 I'd been running; (c) created a specific 3-meeting onboarding arc for mid-HNW with defined outputs at each stage.
4. **Brought in a junior analyst to prepare prospect-specific materials.** Freed me to focus on the human side of the meeting while ensuring each prospect got bespoke analysis.
5. **Tracked leading indicators weekly, not quarterly.** Number of active prospects, time-to-next-touchpoint, conversion-stage progression. Small-cadence visibility caught slippage earlier.
6. **Accepted the compensation consequence.** The missed year's variable was materially down; did not try to argue for protection or re-classification. Clean acceptance made the recovery more credible.

## Result

Year `[N+1]`: closed `[US$64M]` NNM (128% of a `[US$50M]` target; a `[100%]` increase from the prior year). Mid-HNW conversion rate rose to `[25%]`, becoming the volume-driver. UHNW conversion held, supported by the better preparation and fewer concurrent relationships running at max effort. Overall client retention also improved (`[+3pp]`) because the fewer-concurrent-pushes meant more attention to existing relationships. The structured diagnosis process became a framework I've re-applied annually.

## What this demonstrates

**Resilience** — ability to take a significant professional setback without losing confidence or redirecting blame; **Commercial Judgement** — honest cause-diagnosis rather than rationalisation; **Learning Orientation** — translating the miss into a concrete, measurable process change; **Accountability** — accepting the consequences cleanly rather than defending the miss.
