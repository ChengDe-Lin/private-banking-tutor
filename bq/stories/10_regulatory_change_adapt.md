## Task

Triage the book — which clients were materially affected, what each needed, in what priority order — and act decisively without either (a) over-reacting and forcing distressed sales, or (b) under-reacting and letting the risk continue unmanaged.

## Action

1. **Emergency book-review within 48 hours of event.** Listed every client with exposure to the affected product / sleeve; noted position size relative to portfolio, specific issuer, and client's underlying risk tolerance.
2. **Segmented clients into three tiers.**
   - Tier A (immediate call): clients with material exposure (>5% of portfolio) where the embedded risk was now higher than their risk-profile supported. `[Number]` clients.
   - Tier B (within 10 days): clients with moderate exposure (2–5%) — needed a recalibration but not crisis.
   - Tier C (next quarterly review): clients with small exposure (<2%) — maintain, communicate at normal cadence.
3. **Tier A — same-day or next-day individual calls.** Didn't send mass emails; took the 30-minute conversations one by one. Framed honestly: "here's what happened, here's what it means for your specific position, here's what I recommend."
4. **Tier A action plan per client.** Most had one of three outcomes: (a) reduce exposure into a specific new posture (sell part of position into recovering market over `[N]` weeks); (b) reposition to different-structure equivalents (e.g. senior bank debt instead of AT1); (c) in a few cases, documented acknowledgement and hold — specifically where the client was philosophically aligned with the tail risk, reasoned, and had capacity.
5. **Avoided forced sales into depressed prices.** Coordinated with the trading desk on timing — sold into liquidity windows over `[weeks]` rather than dumping into the dislocation.
6. **Re-documented suitability for affected positions.** Each decision — sell, reduce, or hold — got a written memo in the file specifying the updated analysis and client acknowledgement.
7. **Updated my internal screening for the broader book.** Not just AT1 — the episode prompted me to re-review subordinated / callable / hybrid exposures across the book for any other positions with similar tail characteristics. Identified and adjusted `[X]` positions proactively.
8. **Used the moment for client conversations broader than the event.** Several Tier A calls opened into deeper wealth-management discussions — "this is an opportunity to review the full portfolio, not just this piece." Some of those led to expanded mandates.
