# Relationship & Trust: Won a client by listening past their stated objection

> **Template — personalise with your specifics before using.** Replace all `[bracketed]` placeholders with real details from your career.

## Situation

A prospective client — `[describe: e.g. a 58-year-old founder of a mid-cap TW-listed company, introduced via a mutual contact]` — was being courted by three private banks simultaneously, including a competitor with a larger local market share than my firm. In the initial meetings, his stated objection to choosing us was: "Your platform is smaller in TW; I want the biggest relationship that can support my eventual listing / offshore expansion."

## Task

I needed to determine whether the platform-size objection was his *real* concern or a proxy for something deeper — and address the real concern without fighting the surface one. The stakes: `[US$XM]` potential AUM, and a referral chain into his network if won.

## Action

1. **Second meeting, single-topic focus.** Rather than a generalist pitch, I asked him directly: "Tell me specifically what 'bigger platform' means for your situation — what do you need that you're worried we can't deliver?" He listed three things: a specific structured-product capability for a cross-border IPO scenario, relationship continuity if his primary RM left, and a referenceable client in his industry.
2. **Resolved each item concretely.** For the structured-product capability, I brought our regional structuring head to the third meeting with a specific case study from a comparable client. For continuity, I introduced him to my team lead and backup IC so he saw coverage depth, not just me. For industry reference, I asked a satisfied client (with their consent) to take a short call with him.
3. **Didn't compete on platform size.** I explicitly declined to pitch that dimension — "you're right, we're not the largest; here's what we can offer on the dimensions that matter for your situation." That framing removed the dead-end objection from the table.
4. **Respected the decision timeline.** He said 4 weeks; I didn't rush or follow up excessively. Sent one thoughtful follow-up at week 3 with a specific insight relevant to his TW-listed company's sector.

## Result

Won the relationship at `[US$12M]` initial AUM, growing to `[US$22M]` over 18 months as additional liquidity events closed. Most valuably, he referred two network contacts directly to me within 12 months (`[US$8M + US$6M]` additional AUM). Total direct + referred book from this relationship: `[US$36M]` over 18 months.

## What this demonstrates

**Relationship & Trust** — the ability to hear what a client actually needs rather than what they first say, and to address it with specific, verifiable proof points rather than generic reassurance. Commercial discipline in not over-pitching or over-chasing.
