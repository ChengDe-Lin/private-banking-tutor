# Commercial: Cross-sell from a simple FD to full DPM mandate

## Situation

A client came in originally to place a `[US$2M]` USD fixed deposit — "just parking cash until I figure out what to do with it." Straightforward transactional relationship with no expected depth. His profile: `[45-year-old regional sales director at a MNC, recent cash bonus from share-sale event, no prior PB experience]`. Many RMs would treat this as a cash deposit and not develop it further.

## Task

Develop a simple cash relationship into a full wealth-management mandate, without pressuring him or making him feel marketed-to. Understand the underlying reason for the "just parking" stance — often it's lack of trust in any PB, not lack of needs.

## Action

1. **First meeting — no selling.** After documenting the FD, I asked one genuine question: "What's the plan for this money over the next 3–5 years?" He didn't have a clear answer. I offered to send him a one-page framework on how we think about portfolio construction for professionals in his position — no obligation, no follow-up pressure. He said yes.
2. **Sent the one-pager and a curated CIO summary.** Deliberately educational, not salesy. Referenced his specific situation.
3. **Second meeting (90 days later)** — he called to ask questions about the framework. That's the signal: he's engaged. Walked him through a sample SAA for someone at his life stage, emphasised what we *wouldn't* do (no push on specific products) and let him set the pace.
4. **Proposed a staged approach.** Start with `[US$500k]` in a laddered IG bond portfolio — low-touch, transparent, earnings him `[5%]`. Continue the remaining `[US$1.5M]` in FD while he got comfortable with us.
5. **Maintained educational cadence.** Monthly brief note from me on market events, not product pitches. Quarterly short review of the bond ladder.
6. **Year 1.5: he asked about expanding.** Now trust was established. I proposed moving the remaining `[US$1.5M]` FD to a Balanced DPM mandate, plus introduced alternatives (`[US$500k]` committed to PE secondaries). He agreed.
7. **Year 2.5: consolidation.** He brought over `[US$3M]` from another PB where he felt he wasn't getting engagement. Total AUM `[US$5M]`, full DPM mandate, active alts allocations.

## Result

Starting relationship: `[US$2M]` USD FD → ending relationship at 2.5-year mark: `[US$5M]` fully-engaged DPM + advisory mandate with alternatives. Annual revenue grew from minimal (deposit spread only) to `[US$XXk/yr]` across fees + NIM + product. He referred two colleagues (`[US$3M + US$4M]`) over the following year.

## What this demonstrates

**Commercial** — patience and discipline in compounding a small relationship rather than chasing quick quota; **Relationship** — establishing trust through education and non-pressure rather than product-pitching; **Judgement** — reading the signals (when he engaged, when to propose next step) and sizing the pace to the client's comfort.
