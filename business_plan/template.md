# RM Business Plan — Template

> **COACHING NOTE:** Fill this in before the final-round interview. The interviewer will probe every number. Be ready to defend each transferable-% assumption *by name* — not statistically, but relationally: "why will this specific client move with me?" If you can't answer that for every line, your plan isn't ready.
>
> **This is an RM-primary document.** IC candidates typically don't need a formal business plan; if asked, pivot to a "coverage and engagement plan" showing how you'd support a book of RMs. See [`../roles.md`](../roles.md).

The plan rests on three pillars: (1) transfer of my existing high-trust relationships, (2) new acquisition via referral network and centres of influence in `[segment/geography]`, and (3) leveraging `[target firm]`'s specific platform advantages in `[name 2 capabilities]`.

**Transferability rubric notes:**

- **Family / Foundational (100%)** — client has explicitly said they follow you, multi-year deep relationship, family members involved, not the institution.
- **Senior / Long-term (60–90%)** — 5+ year relationship, personal rapport strong, but some institutional dependence (product shelf, banking relationships); each needs a specific conversation.
- **Warm / Transactional (30–60%)** — moderate tenure, specific transactions handled through you but they have other advisors.
- **Cold (10–30%)** — occasional contact, small tickets, likely not follow.

**Critical caveats the interviewer will probe:**

- **Non-solicitation period.** Most PB employment contracts include 6-month to 2-year non-solicitation clauses. During this period I cannot actively solicit my former clients; they must reach out themselves. Affects timing — transfer over 18 months expects client-initiated contact Months 1–6, then active engagement Months 6–18.
- **Credit / approval timing.** New bank's compliance will need to onboard each client independently (full CDD, potentially EDD for some). Realistic onboarding timing: 2–6 weeks per UHNW client, 1–2 weeks per HNW.
- **Product-shelf gaps.** If the target bank doesn't have a specific product my client currently uses (e.g. a specific structured note, specific PE fund access), they may not fully transfer without that fix.

## 7. Risks and dependencies

- **Non-solicitation enforcement.** Current contract's non-solicit period `[6 months]`. Plan assumes client-initiated contact during this window. Need legal sign-off.
- **Referee call quality.** Senior transfers will reach out to my former manager / colleagues for informal reference. Any negative feedback would damage transferable-% estimates.
- **Regulatory approval timelines.** MAS AI opt-in re-verification per client; FATCA documentation transfer. Assume 2–4 weeks per client.
- **Market conditions.** A sharp market drawdown during transition damages client mood, slows new-acquisition, challenges referral generation. Built in a 15% haircut to Year 1 AUM projection for realistic conditions.
- **Platform integration lag.** If target firm has a technology or operational gap affecting client experience, it could delay Year 2 depth.
- **Key-person dependency on me.** Need team-backup structure from Day 1 so clients don't perceive fragility.

## 8. Appendix — one-slide pitch to a prospect

> *Draft a single-slide pitch you'd use in a first prospect meeting. Should contain:*

1. **Who you are** — one line of credentials (role, tenure, segment focus).
2. **What you do differently** — one line of positioning (e.g. "specialise in cross-border structuring for TW / HK families with overseas succession needs").
3. **Signature outcomes** — 2–3 representative (anonymised) client results: e.g. "helped a US$30M client reduce single-stock concentration from 70% to 25% over 3 years without triggering CGT"; "structured a multi-generational trust for a family-office client avoiding US$Xk in succession tax"; "delivered `[X%]` portfolio return net of fees for a Balanced client through a 2-year window including a major market event".
4. **What platform you bring** — 2–3 lines on why the target firm's platform enables your value-proposition for this specific client.
5. **Call to action** — what next step you're inviting from the prospect.
