# 7-Day Study Path

How to use this path — work through one day per sitting. When a concept trips you up, invoke `/project:confusion`. On Day 7, revisit your weakest topic.

> **Before you start:** read [`roles.md`](roles.md) to decide whether you're optimising for a Relationship Manager (RM), Investment Counselor (IC), or PM-Assistant / EAM-Junior interview. That page contains role-emphasised re-orderings of the 7 days below — the ~70% of content is shared but the emphasis and practice priorities diverge.
