## How to use this page

This page exists to rehearse the "walk me through your market view" question. The right preparation:

1. **Rewrite each paragraph yourself** from current sources (your target bank's CIO note, Bloomberg, Reuters).
2. **Date-stamp each claim** — every number gets a "as of _[date]_".
3. **Know the source** — be ready to say "this is CIO house view from the _[month]_ update" or "this is my read on the situation."
4. **Pick one or two tactical trades** that fall out of your view — conviction trades you'd pitch today with a thesis, entry, stop.
5. **Be ready to defend** — interviewers will push back. Know which claims are high-conviction vs informed-guess.

For IC candidates, this page is a rehearsal tool — you should be able to sit down and rewrite it in 60 minutes drawing on your knowledge. For RM candidates, this page is a reference — know enough to reference the house view accurately, without needing to construct it from scratch.
