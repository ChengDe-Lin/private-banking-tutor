## Rates

### Fed (US)

**Current stance (est.):** The Fed has been in a **cutting cycle** since late 2024, bringing the target range from the 2023–2024 peak of **5.25–5.50%** down through 2025 to an estimated **3.75–4.00%** by April 2026.

- **Estimated current Fed funds target (upper bound):** ~**4.00%** ⚠️
- **Market-implied path (SOFR futures):** pricing further modest easing toward **3.25–3.50%** by year-end 2026 — but gap to Fed communication varies.
- **Inflation backdrop:** Core PCE at approximately **2.3–2.7% YoY**, moving toward 2% target slowly; services-ex-housing sticky.
- **Unemployment:** around **4.2–4.5%** — normalised but not recessionary.
- **Key debate:** whether the Fed pauses at current levels vs cuts further — depends on whether Q1 2026 inflation print re-accelerates.

**Interview answer frame:** "The Fed has cut materially from the peak and is now close to neutral on most reasonable estimates of r-star. Market is pricing one or two more cuts in 2026; the CIO view depends on whether you see the last leg of disinflation as self-sustaining. I'd lean slightly hawkish vs market — services CPI remains sticky."

### MAS (Singapore)

**SGD NEER policy band:** MAS has held a **modest appreciation slope** through 2025, easing slightly in the October 2025 MPC meeting as regional disinflation took hold.

- **Estimated current stance (April 2026 MPC):** modest appreciation slope, narrower than 2023 peaks ⚠️
- **Market-implied next move:** potential further slight easing if regional disinflation continues.

### Regional central banks (quick view)

- **HKMA:** pegged to USD; follows Fed mechanically.
- **PBoC:** in easing mode through 2025; reserve requirement cuts and lending-rate reductions; property-sector support ongoing.
- **BoJ:** continued normalisation from the 2024 negative-rate exit; policy rate likely **0.50–0.75%** range by April 2026 ⚠️; potential further hike if wage-growth persists.
- **BoK / RBI / BI:** broadly in easing mode, tracking Fed; Indonesia and India running relatively higher rates given growth dynamics.

## Asia Equities

### China (MSCI China / HSCEI)

- **Narrative:** policy stimulus has continued; property-sector contagion largely absorbed; consumption recovery uneven but improving.
- **Valuation:** cheap on absolute basis (P/E ~10–12x forward ⚠️) but earnings trajectory uncertain.
- **Trade tensions with US** (administration-dependent) remain a tail risk.
- **Most CIO 2026 view:** overweight A-shares with policy-tailwind bias; more cautious on H-shares given dual-listing overhang.
- **Risk:** any escalation in US–China technology / trade restrictions.

### India (NSE / Nifty)

- **Narrative:** strong earnings growth continues; demographic / infrastructure / manufacturing investment themes still valid.
- **Valuation:** premium to EM (P/E ~22–25x forward ⚠️), justified by growth delta.
- **Most CIO view:** overweight structurally; cautious on short-term valuation-derived pullback risk.
- **Risk:** valuation mean-reversion; fiscal position; monsoon / commodity shocks.

### Japan (Nikkei 225 / TOPIX)

- **Narrative:** BoJ normalisation headwind; corporate governance reform tailwind; yen strengthening impacts exporters.
- **Valuation:** fair (P/E ~15–17x forward ⚠️); dividend yield improving.
- **Most CIO view:** neutral to modest overweight; stock selection emphasis — domestic-focused names outperform as yen strengthens.
- **Risk:** BoJ faster normalisation; global growth slowdown hitting exporters.

### Korea (KOSPI)

- **Narrative:** export-cycle dependent; semi-cycle stabilising; corporate governance ("value-up") initiatives gaining traction but slow.
- **Valuation:** cheap (P/E ~10–12x ⚠️) with persistent Korea-discount.
- **Most CIO view:** neutral; tactical opportunities on semi-cycle turns.

### SEA

- **Indonesia:** overweight in some CIO views — demographics, EV-battery supply chain, election clarity; **Jakarta Composite** around all-time-high range ⚠️.
- **Thailand:** political uncertainty weighs; neutral to underweight.
- **Philippines:** demographic tailwind; overweight in some views.
- **Vietnam:** long-term bullish structural; shorter-term FX and regulatory risk.
- **Malaysia:** cheap valuations; limited catalysts.

### Overall Asia ex-Japan

**Most CIO views (early 2026):** neutral to overweight Asia ex-Japan vs developed markets, with India / Indonesia leading and China a tactical call. Regional dispersion high; stock picking matters.

## Key risk themes (early 2026)

1. **Geopolitical:** US–China trade / technology tension; Taiwan strait; Middle East conflict; Russia-Ukraine dynamic. Each is a tail, combined creates persistent risk premium on gold and oil.
2. **Inflation re-acceleration tail:** if Q1 or Q2 CPI prints surprise higher, Fed cutting cycle pauses or partially reverses — bonds sell off, equities re-rate.
3. **Chinese growth disappointment:** stimulus impact muted, property deleveraging incomplete, deflationary pressure persisting.
4. **Credit cycle turn:** IG and HY spreads at tights; any recession brings a meaningful widening.
5. **Private-credit stress:** first real test of the 2015–2025 private-credit growth in a rising-default environment.
6. **Election cycles:** multiple major elections through 2026 create policy uncertainty.
7. **Japan policy normalisation overshoot:** if BoJ hikes faster than markets expect, yen strength disrupts carry trades and equity sectors.
8. **AI-concentration risk:** global equity returns heavily concentrated in AI-exposed mega-caps; any AI thesis pause has broad market implications.
